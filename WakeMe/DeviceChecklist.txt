Device checklist. The simulator cannot prove silent mode.

- Silent mode: alarm still rings with the ringer switch off.
- Focus: alarm still rings while a Focus is on.
- Killing the app mid-mission: backup should ring about 60 seconds later.
- Reboot: a saved alarm still rings after the phone restarts.
- Time zone change: the alarm follows the new local clock time.
- DST: the alarm still fires at the set clock time across the change.
- Low Power Mode: the alarm still rings.
- AlarmKit permission denied: the alarm saves, and the banner says it will not ring.
- A series that crosses midnight: slots before and after midnight all ring.
